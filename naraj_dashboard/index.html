Why this forecast?

Prediction generated from the trained temporal feature set — 71 features built from real CWC rainfall and water-level observations: recent rainfall accumulations (1h–24h), lagged and rolling river levels, multi-station regional signals, and time-of-year encodings. The Random Forest outputs a calibrated probability that the station exceeds its flood threshold in the next 6, 12, or 24 hours.

Per-prediction feature contributions are not currently exposed, so no per-forecast attribution is shown — global feature importances are in “Model & validation info” above.

This is an AI/ML forecast based on historical CWC observations. It is not a live government flood warning.

Understanding FloodWatch

FloodWatch combines river-level records, satellite observations, terrain information and historical flood maps to help people understand flood events.

🌊 Potentially Affected Area

What is it? An area identified by the analysis as potentially affected by flooding.

Remember: It is an analytical estimate and should not be interpreted as an emergency evacuation boundary.

📈 River Water Level

Recorded observations from river monitoring stations.

Satellite Evidence

Radar satellites can observe the surface during day or night and through cloud cover.

Historical Flood Extent

Areas recorded as inundated during past flood events.

⚓ Terrain / Elevation

Information describing relatively higher and lower areas.

📍 Monitoring Station

A location where river or rainfall observations are recorded.
